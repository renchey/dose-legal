Purpose

Meds, Please helps you schedule reminders and keep a personal record of medication you report taking. It does not diagnose conditions, issue prescriptions, recommend medicine or determine whether a missed or extra dose is safe. Always follow the directions given by your clinician, pharmacist and medicine packaging.

Reminders are not guaranteed

Device permissions, battery-saving rules, background limits, network connectivity and operating-system changes can delay or suppress alerts. Meds, Please is not suitable as the sole safeguard for critical medication or emergencies. You are responsible for checking your own treatment instructions and records.

Record accuracy

Entries reflect what you enter. A missing log does not prove a dose was missed, and a logged dose does not independently verify administration. Review times carefully, particularly for late doses that cross midnight.
