One less thing to remember.

Meds, Please provides personal daily medication reminders, an actual-time dose log, and dose history on Android phones and compatible Galaxy Watches.

Why I made this

My partner was far too good at forgetting their daily medication, or taking it at all sorts of different times. I made a little reminder for them. It helped, so I'm sharing it in case it helps you too.

No cost. No ads. No subscriptions. Just a little app that I hope can do a lot of good.

Meds, Please is a reminder and record-keeping tool, not medical advice or an emergency service. Follow your prescribed medication instructions.
