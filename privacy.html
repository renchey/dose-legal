Privacy policy

Meds, Please is a medication reminder and dose-history app developed and published by Reece Woodhams. This policy explains what information Meds, Please handles and what happens when you use its Galaxy Watch companion or export a backup.

Information handled

Meds, Please uses the medication label you provide, its scheduled daily time, and the dates and actual times you report taking medication. It also stores reminder preferences and calculates local statistics. These details may reveal sensitive health information.

Local storage and developer access

Medication schedules and dose history are stored in the app's private storage on your phone or compatible Wear OS watch. Meds, Please does not require an account and does not use any developer-operated server, advertising service or app analytics. The developer does not receive your medication history, sell it or use it for advertising.

Paired Galaxy Watch synchronisation

If you install the optional watch companion, Meds, Please synchronises the schedule and dose records through the Google Play services Wearable Data Layer. This transfer uses Google Play services and may involve Google's technical infrastructure or relay services. Google's own privacy terms apply to Google Play services. The developer has no account or backend that receives this data. Synchronisation can occur when previously disconnected devices reconnect.

Exporting and restoring your records

Using Meds, Please's optional backup feature, you can save an encrypted backup containing your medication label, schedule and logged history to a file location you choose. Meds, Please encrypts the file using AES-GCM with a key derived from a passphrase you set using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. We cannot recover your backup password. You control the file and its storage location. If you choose a cloud storage provider, its separate terms and privacy policy apply. Meds, Please does not automatically upload or collect backup files.

Permissions and reminders

The app requests permission for notifications and Android's Alarms and reminders access, and uses device boot and time-change events to reschedule alerts. These support the core reminder function. Meds, Please does not request your camera, contacts, microphone or precise location.

How long data is kept

Records remain locally until you remove entries, clear application storage, or uninstall Meds, Please. To remove all records, clear Meds, Please storage on every phone and watch where it was installed and delete any backup files you exported. A still-paired device may otherwise resynchronise records from its own local copy. We have no remote medication database to retain or delete.

Children and safety

Meds, Please is not aimed at children. It is a record-keeping and reminder aid, not a diagnostic, prescription, treatment decision or emergency-notification system. If a reminder is missed or late, consult the medicine instructions or your healthcare professional rather than assuming an additional dose is safe.

Policy updates

Changes to features or data practices may require updates to this policy. The date at the top will be revised, and significant changes will be reflected in the app or its Google Play listing.
